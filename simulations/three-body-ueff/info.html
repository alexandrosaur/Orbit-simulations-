🌌 Three-Body Effective Potential

Compute instantaneous gravitational potential energy per body and visualize Ueff every tick.

Overview

This simulation uses a 3-body N-body engine with a separate live graph for the instantaneous effective potential of the selected body. Each body’s Ueff is computed from the other two masses at the current positions.

Learning Objectives

  • See how each body’s potential energy changes as the other bodies move
  • Track instantaneous Ueff without affecting other simulations
  • Use the graph panel to compare bodies and observe energy behavior
  • Learn the difference between per-body potential energy and total system energy

Controls

  • Space — Play / Pause
  • R — Reset simulation
  • S — Step one frame
  • F — Fit 2D view to screen
  • Drag on 2D canvas — Reposition a body / pan
  • Right-click 2D canvas — Add a body
  • Drag on 3D surface — Orbit camera
  • Right-click 3D surface — Pan camera
  • Scroll on 3D surface — Zoom
  • Pitch / Spin / Jib sliders — Adjust 3D camera angle
  • Reset View button — Reset 3D camera to defaults
